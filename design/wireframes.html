SiliconScribe: wireframes (structure only)

Two zones everywhere: a 320 px sidebar for what you ask (prompt, examples, model) and what the agent is doing (run timeline), and a full-height workspace with five tabs. No chat bubbles, no glass cards, one primary action. Orange text = design notes.
